# Contributing to Research Workspace Framework

This framework exists to make context-aware research workspace architecture accessible to asset managers, RIAs, and fintech platforms building investment research tools. Contributions that improve its practical usefulness are welcome.

---

## What We Welcome

- Additional micro-app specifications for research workflows not yet covered
- Asset class specific configurations (commodities, real estate, private markets)
- Implementation patterns for specific technology stacks
- Team collaboration patterns beyond the single-analyst workspace
- Integration guides for common data providers and research platforms

## What We Do Not Accept

- Proprietary or employer-specific implementation details
- Micro-apps that are too narrow to generalise beyond one firm's workflow

## How to Contribute

1. Open an issue describing your proposed addition
2. Fork and implement
3. Submit a pull request with a clear description

## Questions

Open an issue with the `question` tag.
